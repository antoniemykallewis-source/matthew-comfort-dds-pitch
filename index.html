Finding 2 of 3

Show the care behind the result.

What we saw

Add a doctor video and approved cases. Explain the goal, treatment, time and limits in plain words.

Finding 3 of 3

Label both booking routes.

What we saw

The site has online scheduling and an appointment request. Requests need a follow-up.

Why it matters

Patients may not know which route fits them.

Our fix

Say who can book online and who should send a request. Explain the next step before they choose.
