What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

No active ads found.

Meta’s selected practice page showed no active U.S. ads. If current, nearby prospects may meet competitors first. October 20, All ads/Active filters; other pages or countries may differ. See advertiser search ↗

02

12.3 seconds in the test.

Homepage main content appeared after 12.3 seconds in our phone simulation. Visitors may leave before seeing your care. October 20, one slow-4G test; not full-page loading or every visitor’s wait. See mobile test ↗

03

Experience stays buried in text.

The doctor page has a detailed bio but no introduction video. New patients may miss the personal approach behind the credentials. Reviewed page only; not an all-channel video claim. See doctor page ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Cosmetic care lacks a case story.

The cosmetic page describes services without a patient’s before-and-after story. A person comparing dentists has little concrete context for the treatment decision or consultation. See cosmetic page ↗

05

Friday availability disagrees.

Google says Friday 8am–noon; the site says upon request. Patients may expect normal opening hours and arrive without an appointment. October 20 comparison; confirm the intended arrangement. See Google hours ↗

06

A request can look booked.

Online scheduling and appointment requests sit side by side; requests need follow-up. Without clear labels, patients may think a time is reserved or leave for an easier route. See contact page ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

07

The call target differs.

The header number and tap-to-call target differed in one October 20 session. That can create doubt before calling. Call tracking may explain it; no call or routing test was made. See homepage ↗

08

Advice has no clear blog route.

The reviewed navigation does not link to a blog. Patients researching care lack an obvious place for ongoing answers. Navigation gap only; other article URLs may exist. See navigation ↗

09

Your Google reputation is easy to miss.

Homepage testimonials show no Google rating or total. Comparing patients may miss your 4.9 rating and 400 reviews. Homepage and Google observed October 20, 2026. See homepage ↗
